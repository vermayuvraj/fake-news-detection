## 6. Honest statement of what was not done

Listing these explicitly is part of the QA, not a disclaimer.

1. **The PDF was compiled and inspected, but not proofread line by line.**
   MiKTeX was installed locally and the full build cycle runs clean (§3);
   rendered pages were checked for layout, and that check caught two defects a
   static validator cannot see (a TikZ style name colliding with a built-in
   `pgfkeys` key, and an over-wide equation overlapping body text), both since
   fixed. Compiling on Overleaf should reproduce this, but **read the prose
   yourself before submitting** — no automated check substitutes for that.
2. **The transformer control is one architecture at one seed.** DistilBERT was
   fine-tuned and evaluated under all three protocols (plus a 512-token
   replication), so this is no longer an unrun experiment. But it is a single
   seed with no hyperparameter search, and fine-tuning varies across seeds, so
   the manuscript states the *direction* of the effect confidently and the
   *magnitude* loosely. Multi-seed and larger-model runs remain future work.
3. **Cross-corpus evaluation was not run.** Conclusions are therefore scoped to
   this corpus and to corpora built by pairing disjoint publisher sets.
4. **Near-duplicate detection was not implemented.** The 19.37% contamination
   figure is a lower bound, and the paper says so.
5. **Hyperparameters were not tuned.** Deliberate — a single fixed test split
   cannot support a search — and disclosed in Appendix A.
6. **Bibliographic metadata was not machine-verified** (§4).
